---
title: "A private place to track your job search"
date: 2026-11-09
category: new
description: "Bring opportunities from Provn and beyond together in your applications tracker."
---

- Save jobs you find outside Provn by pasting a link and reviewing the details, or enter them manually. Saving a job does not submit an application.
- Search, filter, and export your opportunities together, and manage the status of jobs you’ve saved from elsewhere.
- Provn applications show their actual application status in the same tracker. Archiving an opportunity only changes your personal tracker.
- Improved dates and navigation for older Provn applications.
