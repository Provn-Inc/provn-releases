---
title: "More flexibility in resume scoring"
date: 2026-11-18
category: improved
description: "Choose a dedicated resume rubric, separate from the rubric used to evaluate a challenge."
---

- Employers can use a separate rubric to assess resumes when reviewing applicants, while keeping challenge evaluations distinct.
- Existing jobs keep their current scoring setup; new jobs can use the separate resume rubric by default.
