date: 2026-07-31
category: improved
description: "More helpful emails, better application ordering, and faster applicant lists."

- Candidates receive an email with their next step when their resume fit score is ready, once per job.
- Candidates receive an email when their feedback report becomes available.
- Applications sort newest first within each status group, matching the application dates shown.
- Applicant lists load faster for jobs with more than 100 applicants.
