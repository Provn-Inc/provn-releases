date: 2026-10-27
category: new
description: "Import job descriptions, organize applications by status, and control your public profile."

- Import jobs by uploading a document or pasting text.
- The job import screen warns before you leave with unsaved work.
- Fixed timeouts when importing large numbers of jobs.
- Applications are organized into Draft, Applied, and Closed tabs.
- Public profiles are separate from the profile editor, with controls to choose which challenges to show or hide.
- A challenge best-practices guide is available under the Proving Ground menu.
- Fixed company pages overflowing on smaller screens.
- Job deadline dates are easier to read on mobile.
- Fixed leaderboard links opening the wrong candidate details.
